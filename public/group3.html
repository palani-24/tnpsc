TNPSC Group 3 & 3A Examination

Combined Civil Services Examination III for Subordinate Services and Junior Inspector posts in state departments.

Official Gazette Copy
Important Advisory for Group 3: Group 3 & 3A exam details are presented here as an informational category. Vacancies and exact exam patterns are published periodically by TNPSC. Aspirants are strongly advised to verify current year notification at tnpsc.gov.in before beginning preparation.

Overview & Job Roles under Group 3

Group 3 recruitments cater to subordinate technical and administrative cadres, such as Junior Inspector of Cooperative Societies and Store-Keeper Grade II.

Designation Department Duties
Junior Inspector of Cooperative Societies Cooperation Dept Field verification and auditing of local cooperative societies.
Store-Keeper Grade II Industries & Commerce Inventory management and stock record maintenance.

Exam Structure

Single Written Exam (Objective - 300 Marks)

• 100 Questions: General Tamil / General English (SSLC Standard)
• 75 Questions: General Studies (HSC/SSLC Standard)
• 25 Questions: Aptitude & Mental Ability
