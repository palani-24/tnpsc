Cadre Posts & Department Pay Scale Matrix

Group 3 offers rapid promotion paths to Senior Inspector and Sub-Registrar / Cooperative Officer cadres.

Cadre Category Post Title Department / Service Pay Matrix Level Educational Qualification
Group 3A Junior Inspector of Cooperative Societies Cooperative Department Level 10 (₹20,600 - ₹75,900) HSC (+2 Pass) or Diploma in Cooperation. Higher preference for B.Com (Cooperation).
Group 3A Store-Keeper Grade-II Industries & Commerce Dept Level 10 (₹20,600 - ₹75,900) HSC (+2 Pass) with experience in store accounting.
Group 3 Executive Station Fire Officer Fire & Rescue Services Dept Level 11 (₹20,600 - ₹85,600) Bachelor's Degree in Science / Engineering + Physical Fitness standards.

Single Written OMR Examination Pattern

Part A: Tamil Language

General Tamil (100 Questions)

  • Marks: 150 Marks (SSLC Standard)
  • Compulsory Minimum: 40% (60 Marks) required for Part B evaluation.
  • Covers இலக்கணம், இலக்கியம், தமிழ் அறிஞர்களும் தமிழ்த் தொண்டும்.
Part B: General Studies & Aptitude

GS (75 Qs) + Aptitude (25 Qs)

  • General Studies: 75 Questions (HSC Standard) - 112.5 Marks
  • Aptitude & Mental Ability: 25 Questions (SSLC Standard) - 37.5 Marks
  • Total Exam Duration: 3 Hours | Total Marks: 300 Marks
