Senior HR&CE Administrative Cadre

TNPSC Group 7A Examination

Direct recruitment for Executive Officer Grade-I in Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments (HR&CE) Department (Gazetted Level Officer).

Download 2023 Paper PDF Official Notification
Religious Criteria
Must Profess Hindu Religion
Qualification
Degree in Law (LL.B) or B.A / B.Sc / B.Com
Age Limit
30 - 37 Yrs (Relaxed for Reserved)
Pay Scale
Level 18 (₹36,900 - ₹1,35,100)
Selection Stage
Written Examination + Interview

Cadre Posts & Department Pay Scale Matrix

Executive Officer Grade-I officers administer major Category-A temples, managing crore-budget temple endowments and legal litigations.

Post Title Department / Service Pay Matrix Level Starting Basic Pay Qualification & Preference
Executive Officer Grade-I HR&CE Department (Class 1 Gazetted) Level 18 ₹36,900 / month Degree in Arts / Science / Commerce. First preference given to Degree in Law (LL.B) graduates. Must profess Hindu religion.

Selection Pattern: Written Papers + Interview

Paper I & II: GS & Saivism / Vaishnavism

General Studies & Hindu Religion

  • Paper I: General Studies + Tamil: 300 Marks
  • Paper II: சைவமும் வைணவமும்: 300 Marks
Paper III & Interview

Law & Oral Test

  • Paper III: Law & HR&CE Act 1959: Hindu Law, Temple Endowments & Property Act (300 Marks).
  • Interview / Oral Test: 60 Marks
