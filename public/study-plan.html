Beginner TNPSC Study Plans & Timetables

Structured day-by-day timetables designed for beginners, working professionals, and full-time aspirants.

30-Day Revision Sprint Rapid Revision

Designed for aspirants taking the exam in 1 month or doing last-minute syllabus recap.

Daily Time Split (6 Hours):
  • 2 Hours: General Tamil / English 100 Qs Revision
  • 2 Hours: Unit 8 & Unit 9 High-Yield Topics
  • 1 Hour: Aptitude & Mental Ability Practice
  • 1 Hour: Full-length Mock Tests & PYQs
90-Day Standard Plan Recommended for Beginners

Complete coverage of Samacheer school books (6th to 10th) + General Studies Units 1 to 10.

3-Phase Roadmap:
  • Days 1 - 45: Samacheer School Books 6th to 10th Standard
  • Days 46 - 75: Unit-wise GS Topics + Aptitude Formulae
  • Days 76 - 90: PYQ Solving & 10 Model Grand Tests
180-Day Comprehensive Group 1 & 2 Mains

Deep coverage for Group 1 Prelims + Mains Descriptive Answer Writing practice.

Mains Focus:
  • Daily 1 Hour Tamil Eligibility Paper Writing
  • Social Issues, Science & Tech, Indian Polity
  • Current Affairs & Editorial Analysis
