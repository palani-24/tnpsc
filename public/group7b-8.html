HR&CE Department Services

TNPSC Group 7B & Group 8 Examination

Direct recruitment for Executive Officer Grade-III (Group 7B) and Executive Officer Grade-IV (Group 8) in Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments (HR&CE) Department.

Download 2023 Paper PDF Official Notification
Religious Qualification
Must Profess Hindu Religion
Qualification
Degree (EO Grade III) | SSLC (EO Grade IV)
Age Limit
25 - 37 Yrs (Relaxed for Reserved)
Pay Scale
Level 10 - 11 (₹20,600 - ₹85,600)
Core Paper
Paper II: சைவமும் வைணவமும்

Post Breakdown & Qualification Criteria

Executive Officers manage temple administration, land leases, and religious festivals in HR&CE temples.

Exam Category Post Title Department Pay Matrix Level Educational Qualification
Group 7B Executive Officer Grade-III HR&CE Department Level 11 (₹20,600 - ₹85,600) Bachelor's Degree in any discipline. Must profess Hindu religion.
Group 8 Executive Officer Grade-IV HR&CE Department Level 10 (₹20,600 - ₹75,900) SSLC (10th Pass) or equivalent. Must profess Hindu religion.

Exam Scheme: Paper I & Paper II (சைவமும் வைணவமும்)

Paper I: General Studies & Tamil

Paper I (200 Qs / 300 Marks)

  • General Tamil (100 Qs): 150 Marks
  • General Studies (75 Qs) + Aptitude (25 Qs): 150 Marks
  • Duration: 3 Hours
Paper II: Special Religion Paper

Paper II: சைவமும் வைணவமும் (300 Marks)

  • Saivism (100 Qs): 12 Thirumurai, 63 Nayanmars, Saiva Siddhantam philosophy.
  • Vaishnavism (100 Qs): 12 Alwars, 4000 Divya Prabandham, Temple Agamas & HR&CE Act 1959.
