Group 2 & Group 2A Overview

Group 2 (Interview Posts) and Group 2A (Non-Interview Posts) recruit graduates for executive state cadre posts like Sub-Registrar, Revenue Inspector, Municipal Commissioner, and Senior Inspectors in cooperatives.

Why Group 2 is Popular?

Offers maximum vacancy seats across Tamil Nadu government departments with fast promotions and strong administrative power.

General Tamil / General English Weightage

The 100-question language section forms 50% of the total preliminary score, making Samacheer language books vital.

Job Roles under Group 2 & 2A

Designation Category Key Duties & Department
Sub-Registrar Grade II Group 2 (Interview) Land and property document registrations, stamp duty audit.
Municipal Commissioner Grade II Group 2 (Interview) Civic administration and municipal scheme execution.
Revenue Inspector (RI) Group 2A (Non-Interview) Field revenue surveys, income & community certificate verification.
Senior Inspector of Cooperative Societies Group 2A (Non-Interview) Auditing cooperative banks and societies.
Assistant Section Officer (ASO) Group 2 / 2A Policy research and file drafting in TN Secretariat.

Exam Structure

Preliminary Exam (Objective - 300 Marks)

• 100 Questions: General Tamil / General English (SSLC Standard)
• 75 Questions: General Studies (Degree Standard)
• 25 Questions: Aptitude & Mental Ability (SSLC Standard)

Subjects to Focus

  • General Tamil / General English (6th to 12th Samacheer Kalvi)
  • Unit 8: History, Culture & Socio-Political Movements in TN
  • Unit 9: Development Administration in TN
  • Aptitude and Mental Ability (Simplification, Percentage, HCF/LCM, Ratio)
  • Indian Polity & Governance

Beginner Preparation Tips

Target 95+ in Language: Thoroughly memorize 6th to 10th Tamil/English textbooks.
Daily 25 Aptitude Practice: Practice Aptitude questions daily to secure full 25 marks.
