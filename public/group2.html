Combined Civil Services II (CCS II)

TNPSC Group 2 & Group 2A Examination

Direct recruitment for Executive (Group 2 with Interview) and Non-Executive (Group 2A without Interview) cadres across Tamil Nadu Revenue, Commercial Taxes, Secretariat, and Registration departments.

Selection Pattern for Group 2 & Group 2A

Common Prelims OMR

Prelims Exam (200 Qs / 300 Marks)

  • Part A: General Tamil (100 Qs) - SSLC Standard (150 Marks)
  • Part B: General Studies (75 Qs) - Degree Standard (112.5 Marks)
  • Part C: Aptitude & Mental Ability (25 Qs) - SSLC Standard (37.5 Marks)
  • Duration: 3 Hours | Min Qualifying Marks: 90 Marks
Group 2 Mains & Interview

Descriptive Mains + Interview

  • Paper I: Tamil Eligibility Test (Descriptive 100 Marks, Qual. 40)
  • Paper II: General Studies Descriptive (300 Marks, 3 Hours)
  • Interview / Oral Test: 40 Marks
  • Total Merit: 340 Marks (300 Mains + 40 Interview)
Group 2A Mains Only

Descriptive Mains (No Interview)

  • Paper I: Tamil Eligibility Test (Qualifying 40 Marks)
  • Paper II: General Studies (300 Marks Descriptive/Objective)
  • No Interview Stage! Post allocation made directly based on Paper II marks.
