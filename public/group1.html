TNPSC Group 1 Examination Overview

TNPSC Group 1 is the flagship examination conducted by the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission. Successful candidates enter the state civil executive service at high-ranking administrative posts such as Deputy Collector and Deputy Superintendent of Police.

Who Should Choose Group 1?

Graduates with strong analytical skills, passion for public governance, willingness to commit to structured 6-12 months preparation, and administrative leadership mindset.

Power & Responsibilities

High executive power, sub-divisional magisterial authority, law enforcement leadership, and direct role in executing state development welfare programs.

Job Designations under Group 1 Cadre

Designation Department Key Administrative Duties
Deputy Collector (Category I) Revenue Dept Sub-divisional magistrate, revenue collection, disaster management head.
Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) Police Service Sub-divisional law & order, crime investigation, policing leadership.
Assistant Commissioner (Commercial Taxes) Commercial Taxes State revenue administration, GST auditing, tax enforcement.
District Registrar Registration Dept Property registration enforcement, stamp duty auditing.
Assistant Director of Rural Development Rural Development Panchayat Raj governance, rural infrastructure schemes.

3-Stage Selection Process & Marks Distribution

Stage 1: Preliminary Exam (Objective MCQ)

200 Questions | 300 Marks | 3 Hours

• 175 Questions: General Studies (Degree Standard)
• 25 Questions: Aptitude and Mental Ability (SSLC Standard)

Stage 2: Main Written Exam (Descriptive Papers)

750 Marks Total

• Paper I: Tamil Eligibility Test (Qualifying - 100 Marks)
• Paper II: General Studies (250 Marks)
• Paper III: General Studies (250 Marks)
• Paper IV: General Studies (250 Marks)

Stage 3: Oral Test / Interview

100 Marks

Assesses candidate's personality, decision-making ability, awareness of state socio-economic issues, and leadership traits.

Official Units to Master for Group 1

90-Day Step-by-Step Preparation Strategy

Month 1: School Textbooks & Base Building

Cover 6th to 10th Samacheer Kalvi Science and Social Science textbooks thoroughly. Practice 30 mins daily Aptitude.

Month 2: Unit 8, Unit 9 & Indian Polity

Focus heavily on Tamil Nadu History, Thirukkural concepts, state welfare schemes, and Lakshmi Kanth Indian Polity chapters.

Month 3: Test Series & Past Paper Practice

Solve past 5 years official Group 1 preliminary question papers. Target scoring 140+ correct answers out of 200.

Group 1 Previous Year Question Papers & Curated Notes

Beginner Tips & Strategy

1. Focus on High Weightage Units: Unit 8, Unit 9, Indian Polity, and Aptitude contribute over 110 questions out of 200 in Prelims.
2. Start Descriptive Answer Writing Early: Don't wait until Prelims results to begin Mains preparation. Practice 2 answers daily.
3. Stay Updated on Tamil Nadu Affairs: Read daily Tamil newspapers (Dina Mani / Hindu Tamil) for state development schemes.
