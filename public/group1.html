TNPSC Group 1 Examination

TNPSC Group 1 is the highest civil service competitive examination conducted by Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission for direct recruitment of Deputy Collectors, DSPs, Commercial Tax Officers, and District Registrars.

Prelims -> Mains -> Interview

3-Stage Selection Scheme

Stage 1: Screening

Preliminary Examination (OMR)

  • Total Questions: 200 Questions (Objective OMR)
  • General Studies (Degree Standard): 175 Questions
  • Aptitude & Mental Ability (SSLC Std): 25 Questions
  • Total Marks: 300 Marks (1.5 Marks per Q)
  • Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
  • Negative Marking: NO Negative Marks
  • Ratio for Mains: Top 1:20 Candidates called for Mains
Stage 2: Main Written

Main Written Examination (Descriptive)

  • Paper I: Tamil Eligibility Test (Descriptive 100 Marks, Qual. 40 Marks)
  • Paper II: Modern History, Indian Culture & Social Issues (250 Marks)
  • Paper III: Indian Polity, Science & Tech, Environment (250 Marks)
  • Paper IV: Indian Economy, Geography & TN Admin (250 Marks)
  • Total Mains Marks: 750 Marks (Papers II + III + IV)
Stage 3: Interview

Oral Test & Personality Interview

  • Maximum Interview Marks: 90 Marks
  • Interview Board: TNPSC Chairman & Expert Panel
  • Grand Total for Merit Ranking: 840 Marks (750 Mains + 90 Interview)
  • Final Merit List: Post allocation based on rank and options filled during counseling.

Group 1 Prelims Syllabus Units & School Book Mapping

Unit I: General Science

15 Questions

Scientific Concepts, Physics laws, Chemistry elements, Acids & Bases, Botany, Human Physiology, Environment & Ecology.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 6th to 10th Science & 11th/12th Biology / Chemistry.

Unit II: Current Events

15 Questions

State & National Appointments, Government Schemes, Awards, Sports, Defense, Summits, Science Innovations.

Unit III: Geography of India

15 Questions

Location, Physical Features, Monsoon, Rainfall, Rivers, Soil, Minerals, Natural Vegetation, Transport & Disaster Management.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 10th Geography Vol 1 & 2, 11th Geography.

Unit IV: History & Culture of India

15 Questions

Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic Period, Guptas, Delhi Sultans, Mughals, Marathas, South Indian Kingdoms & Architecture.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 6th & 11th History Textbooks.

Unit V: Indian Polity

25 Questions

Constitution of India, Preamble, Fundamental Rights & Duties, Union Executive, Parliament, Judiciary, Panchayat Raj, RTI, Lokpal.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 11th & 12th Political Science + Indian Polity by M. Laxmikanth.

Unit VI: Indian Economy

15 Questions

NITI Aayog, Five Year Plans, RBI Monetary Policy, GST, Agriculture, Banking, Rural Development & Poverty Alleviation.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 11th & 12th Economics.

Unit VII: Indian National Movement

15 Questions

1857 Revolt, INC Formation, Swadeshi Movement, Gandhian Era, Partition of India, Freedom Fighters of Tamil Nadu.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 10th & 12th History.

Unit VIII: History, Culture, Heritage & Socio-Political Movements in TN

40 Questions (High Weightage)

Sangam Literature, Archaeological sites (Keezhadi), Thirukkural 20 Adhigarams, Self-Respect Movement, Dravidian Movement, Periyar, Anna.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 6th-12th Tamil & 11th Ethics (அறவியலும் இந்தியப் பண்பாடும்).

Unit IX: Development Administration in Tamil Nadu

30 Questions (High Weightage)

Human Development Index in TN, e-Governance, Social Welfare Schemes, Education & Health Systems, Industrial Growth in TN.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 11th Economics & TN Policy Notes.

Unit X: Aptitude & Mental Ability

25 Questions

Simplification, HCF & LCM, Ratio, Percentage, Simple & Compound Interest, Area & Volume, Time & Work, Logical Reasoning.

Recommended Samacheer Kalvi: 6th to 10th School Maths Textbooks.
