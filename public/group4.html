TNPSC Group 4 & VAO Examination (Code: 496)

The largest competitive examination in Tamil Nadu conducted for direct recruitment to Village Administrative Officer (VAO), Junior Assistant, Typist, Steno-Typist, and Bill Collector posts.

Download 2024 Paper PDF Code 496 Gazette
Exam Qualification
SSLC (10th Pass)
Age Limit
21 - 42 Yrs (VAO) | 18 - 37 Yrs (JA/Typist)
Pay Scale
Level 8 - 10 (₹19,500 - ₹71,900)
Question Scheme
200 Qs | 300 Marks | 3 Hours
Selection
Single OMR Test + Certificate Verification

Group 4 Post Breakdown & Technical Requirements

Cadrewise salary scale, department allocation, and typewriting/shorthand qualification criteria:

Post Title Department Pay Level Min Education Technical / Typewriting Qualification
Village Administrative Officer (VAO) Revenue Department Level 10 (₹20,600 - ₹75,900) SSLC (10th Pass) No technical qualification needed. Knowledge of Tamil compulsory.
Junior Assistant (Non-Security) Various State Govt Depts Level 8 (₹19,500 - ₹71,900) SSLC (10th Pass) General SSLC Pass. Computer Automation Certificate preferred.
Junior Assistant (Security) Treasuries & Accounts Dept Level 8 (₹19,500 - ₹71,900) SSLC (10th Pass) Security deposit required upon joining.
Typist State Secretariats & Depts Level 8 (₹19,500 - ₹71,900) SSLC (10th Pass) Typewriting Higher Grade in Tamil AND English (or Higher in one & Lower in other).
Steno-Typist Grade-III State Judicial & Administrative Services Level 10 (₹20,600 - ₹75,900) SSLC (10th Pass) Typewriting Higher + Shorthand Higher Grade in Tamil & English.
Bill Collector Grade-I Town Panchayats & Revenue Level 8 (₹19,500 - ₹71,900) SSLC (10th Pass) General SSLC Qualification.

Group 4 Code 496 Official Exam Pattern

Part A: General Tamil

Part A: General Tamil (100 Questions)

  • Marks: 150 Marks (1.5 Marks per Question)
  • Qualifying Cutoff: 40% (60 Marks) mandatory for Part B scoring evaluation.
  • Covers Part A (இலக்கணம்), Part B (இலக்கியம்), Part C (தமிழ் அறிஞர்களும் தமிழ்த் தொண்டும்).
Part B: General Studies & Aptitude

Part B: GS (75 Qs) + Aptitude (25 Qs)

  • General Studies (75 Qs): SSLC Standard (112.5 Marks)
  • Aptitude & Mental Ability (25 Qs): SSLC Standard (37.5 Marks)
  • Total Exam Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
  • Total Marks: 300 Marks | Negative Marking: ZERO
