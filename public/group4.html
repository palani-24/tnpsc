TNPSC Group 4 & VAO Overview

Combined Civil Services Examination IV (Group IV Services - Exam Code: 496) is conducted for recruitment to entry-level administrative, revenue, clerical, and stenographic posts across Tamil Nadu state government departments.

Why Group 4 is Special?

10th standard SSLC qualification eligibility opens up tens of thousands of state government job opportunities every year for students across urban and rural Tamil Nadu.

High Scoring General Tamil Section

Part C consists of 100 General Tamil questions (150 Marks). Scoring 95+ in Tamil guarantees selection in the state top rank list.

Cadre & Job Posts under Group 4

Post Name Department Key Responsibilities
Village Administrative Officer (VAO) Revenue Dept Village administration, land record maintenance, birth/death/caste certificate verification, local law & order coordination.
Junior Assistant (Non-Security & Security) Various State Depts Office administration, file management, public counter operations, government scheme dispatch.
Typist Various Depts Official letter typing in Tamil and English, order drafting, document digitization.
Steno-Typist Grade III Various Depts Stenography dictation taking, confidential correspondence, officer assistance.
Bill Collector Grade I Rural Development Local Panchayat tax collection, revenue record updating, local body accounting.

Official Code 496 Exam Pattern (300 Marks)

Part A: General Studies (SSLC Standard)

75 Questions | 112.5 Marks

Covering General Science (5 Qs), Geography (5 Qs), History/INM (10 Qs), Indian Polity (15 Qs), Indian Economy & TN Dev Admin (20 Qs), and TN Culture & Thirukkural (20 Qs).

Part B: Aptitude and Mental Ability (SSLC Standard)

25 Questions | 37.5 Marks

15 Aptitude Questions (Simplification, Percentage, HCF/LCM, Ratio, Interest, Area/Volume) + 10 Reasoning Questions (Puzzles, Dice, Visual, Number Series).

Part C: தமிழ் தகுதி மற்றும் மதிப்பீட்டுத் தேர்வு (General Tamil)

100 Questions | 150 Marks

7 Units: இலக்கணம் (25 Qs), சொல்லகராதி (15 Qs), எழுதும் திறன் (15 Qs), கலைச் சொற்கள் (10 Qs), வாசித்தல் திறன் (15 Qs), எளிய மொழிபெயர்ப்பு (5 Qs), இலக்கியம் & தமிழ் அறிஞர்கள் (15 Qs).

90-Day Group 4 Preparation Plan

Month 1: 6th to 10th Tamil Textbooks Mastery

Read 6th to 10th Samacheer Tamil textbooks page-by-page. Focus on இலக்கணம், தமிழ் அறிஞர்கள், and 20 திருக்குறள் அதிகாரங்கள்.

Month 2: Aptitude Practice & General Studies

Solve 25 Aptitude questions daily. Cover 6th to 10th Social Science (History, Civics, Geography, Economy).

Month 3: OMR Mock Tests & PYQs

Attempt past 5 years official Group 4 question papers. Practice filling OMR sheets in 3 hours time limit.
