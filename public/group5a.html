TNPSC Group 5A Secretariat Examination

Specialized recruitment for Assistant Section Officer (ASO) in Tamil Nadu Secretariat Service (Other than Law & Finance). Key administrative hub for cabinet policy note drafting.

Secretariat Cadre Posts & Promotion Hierarchy

Group 5A ASOs directly handle Cabinet Notes, Government Orders (G.O.), and Assembly Questions.

Post Title Department / Department Posting Pay Level Key Administrative Duties Career Promotion Hierarchy
Assistant Section Officer (ASO) TN Secretariat Service (Fort St. George) Level 16 (₹36,400 - ₹1,15,700) Policy drafting, examining department files, scrutiny of Assembly Questions & Cabinet notes. ASO -> Section Officer (SO) -> Under Secretary (US) -> Deputy Secretary (DS) -> Joint Secretary.

Exam Scheme & Paper Syllabus

Paper I: Tamil & Secretariat Office Manual

Paper I (150 Marks | 3 Hours)

  • General Tamil Language: Grammar, Translation & Precis Writing.
  • Secretariat Office System: Office Manual Rules, File Indexing, and Note-Sheet preparation.
Paper II: English & Drafting

Paper II (150 Marks | 3 Hours)

  • General English Drafting: Official Letter Writing, Memo drafting & Summarization.
  • General Knowledge & Aptitude: Indian Constitution, TN Administration & Logical Reasoning.
