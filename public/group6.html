Tamil Nadu Forest Service

TNPSC Group 6 Examination (ACF)

Direct recruitment for Assistant Conservator of Forests (ACF) in Tamil Nadu Forest Department (Class 1 Gazetted Service).

Download 2023 Paper PDF Official Portal
Exam Level
Class 1 Forest Gazetted
Qualification
B.Sc Forestry / Science / Engineering
Age Limit
21 - 34 Yrs (Relaxed for SC/ST)
Pay Scale
Level 22 (₹56,100 - ₹2,05,700)
Selection
Written + Walking Test + Interview

Physical Fitness & Endurance Test Standards

Candidates qualifying the written test must pass the compulsory 25 km / 16 km Walking Endurance Test:

Gender Category Minimum Height Chest Normal / Expanded Walking Endurance Test Standard
Male Candidates 163 cm 84 cm (5 cm expansion required) 25 Kilometers walk within 4 Hours
Female Candidates 150 cm 79 cm (5 cm expansion required) 16 Kilometers walk within 4 Hours

Selection Pattern & Optional Papers

Paper I & II: GS & Optional 1

General Studies & Optional Paper 1

  • Paper I General Studies: Degree Standard (200 Marks)
  • Paper II Optional Subject 1: Forestry / Botany / Agriculture / Zoology (300 Marks)
Paper III & Interview

Optional Paper 2 & Interview

  • Paper III Optional Subject 2: Environmental Science / Chemistry / Engineering (300 Marks)
  • Interview / Oral Test: 120 Marks
